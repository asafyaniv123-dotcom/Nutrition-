# Root words from Sanskrit, Hindi, Pali and Tamil

Research for an invented name for the evening-reflection app (the logo is a small sun). The themes are body and mind, the day, light and sun, reflection, presence, calm, wholeness, memory, and becoming.

**How to read this.**

- **Lang:** Skt = Sanskrit, Hi = Hindi, Pa = Pali, Ta = Tamil.
- **Say it:** CAPITALS mark the stressed syllable. Sanskrit has no fixed word stress, so the stress shown is the natural one for an international speaker.
- **Score** rates the word as a *building block*, not as a finished name. 5 means open CV syllables, no clusters, no sounds missing in Japanese, Arabic or Spanish, and no bad meaning. 1 means avoid it.
- **Clashes** were checked in English (EN), Spanish (ES), Portuguese (PT), Hebrew (HE), Arabic (AR) and Japanese (JA). A dash means I found nothing worth noting. Cells marked *(memory)* are my own language knowledge and were not web-verified.
- A trademark search (USPTO/EUIPO/App Store) is still needed for whichever blend wins. The brand column only names the well-known uses.

**Not included, and why.** I left out *ātman* (soul), *bodhi* (awakening), *dhyāna* (meditation), *soma* (sacred drink and moon; also the drug in *Brave New World*) and *Aditya*/*Mitra* (deity names). Each is too sacred, too clustered, or too taken.

**Two extra finds worth knowing about:**

- Bengali *alo* (আলো) means "light", but **Alo Yoga** owns it in wellness.
- *nilaya* (निलय) is a real Sanskrit word meaning "dwelling, refuge". It turns up again in blend 7 below.

## Eight two-root blends

| # | Blend | Say it | Built from | What it says | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Olimei** | o-li-MAY | oli + mei | light + body/truth: "the light on your body", "the truth in the light" | Two diphthong-free halves; reads cleanly in every script. Check app-store collisions. |
| 2 | **Kayoli** | ka-YO-li | kaya + oli | body + light | Softens *kaya* enough to lose the cannabis echo. JA *kayori* is fine. |
| 3 | **Velina** | ve-LEE-na | vela + ina | time/the hour + the sun: "the sun's hour", the evening | An existing Bulgarian/Slavic given name. Arabic says *Felina* (cat-ish). |
| 4 | **Rasayu** | ra-SA-yu | rasa + ayu | essence + life: "the taste of a life" | Near *rasāyana*, Ayurveda's rejuvenation therapy. That is a plus in India and invisible elsewhere. |
| 5 | **Arkaya** | ar-KA-ya | arka + kaya | sun + body: the logo and the body in one word | The *rk* cluster is the only friction. |
| 6 | **Manoli** | ma-NO-li | mana + oli | mind + light | A Greek given name (Manolis). Fine in HE (מנה, "portion"). |
| 7 | **Nilaya** | ni-LA-ya | nila + ayu | moon + life, the evening side of the day | **An existing Sanskrit word, "dwelling, refuge"**, which is warm but means less is owned. Asian Paints sells a "Nilaya" wallpaper line *(memory)*. |
| 8 | **Inayu** | i-NA-yu | ina + ayu | sun + life | Very soft and all open syllables. Ina is weak on its own, so the meaning is carried by the story, not the sound. |
