# Name screen, round 15: six invented words

Screened 24 Oct 2026. This is not legal advice and not a clearance search.

Candidates: Dialumo, Seralume, Sunela, Heliami, Diami, Serami. They passed the
store and domain screen in `round15.log`: no App Store hit, no Play hit. Heliami's
YouTube handle is taken. Store title in every case: "<Name>: Daily Reflection".
The brief: one invented word, like Duolingo. Meaningful roots, open syllables,
ending on a vowel.

## What was searched, and what could not be

- **US trademarks:** Trademarkia quoted search, `?q=%22<name>%22`, for each name
  and for one-letter-off spellings.
  - **Calibration:** the quoted search returned the real marks SERALUME, SERAMI
    and DIAMI, so its zeros are real zeros for the exact spelling. Trademarkia's
    "may be available" banner is boilerplate and was ignored.
  - **Near spellings searched:** dialuma, dialume, dialumi · seralumi, serolume ·
    sunella, sunelle, sunila · helami, heliani, "helio me" · diamy, dyami, diame ·
    cerami, seramy, serame, seremi.
  - **Bare root `LUME`:** 35 marks, read for context on Seralume.
- **Not reachable from this session:**
  - EUIPO TMview (`tmdn.org/tmview/...`) returned an empty page (JS app).
  - UK IPO (`trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmtext`) returned **HTTP 403**.
  - WIPO Global Brand Database (`branddb.wipo.int`) returned only an ALTCHA bot check.
  - A web search for EUIPO records of Diami, Serami and Sunela found nothing.
  - **So EU, UK, WIPO/Madrid, Japan (J-PlatPat) and China (CNIPA) are NOT
    covered.** Run the finalist through TMview, the WIPO Global Brand Database
    and J-PlatPat by hand before filing.
- **Existing use:** a general web search for each quoted name, follow-up
  searches on the hits, and an App Store / Play search restricted to
  apps.apple.com and play.google.com. That search found no app with any of the
  six names, but the engine indexes the stores thinly, so its zero is weak evidence.
- **Pronunciation and meaning** come from knowledge of the languages, checked
  against the web where a claim was specific (Hindi *sunehla*, *Dyami*, Arabic
  *سيراميك*). Transliterations are suggestions. **A native speaker of each
  language should confirm them before a decision.**

Verdicts:
- **CLEAR:** nothing relevant found.
- **CAUTION:** a similar mark, an unhelpful reading, or a name already in use,
  explained in the section.
- **CONFLICT:** the identical mark is live, or it sits in a class that matters
  (9/41/42/44/45), or it is dominant enough to own the search results.

Sound rating: 1 (awkward) to 5 (rolls off the tongue). Stress is shown in capitals.

## 1. Dialumo: día + lumo (day + light)

**Trademarks: no exact mark found**
- `"dialumo"`: **0 results.**
  https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=%22dialumo%22
- `"dialume"`: 2 results, both DIALUME from Armour Pharmaceutical, class 5,
  **dead**: 81029878 (cancelled 1982) and 73732909 (cancelled 2010). The goods
  were an antacid for heartburn and sour stomach. Not in 9/41/42/44/45.
  https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=%22dialume%22
- `"dialuma"` 0, `"dialumi"` 0.

**Existing use**
- No company, product, person or place found.
- The search engine corrected the name to **dialumene**, an aluminium Al=Al
  compound with active chemistry papers in 2017–2026. The Google results for the
  bare root are chemistry, not a brand: nobody owns them, and they are not
  ours either. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dialumene
- No app found.

**Sound and meaning**
| language | said as | written | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | dee-uh-LOO-moh, often **DIAL**-oo-moh | Dialumo | reads as *dial* + *-umo*, so the "day" root is lost. Faint *aluminium* echo. |
| German | di-a-LU-mo | Dialumo | clean. *Dia* is also a photo slide in German. |
| Spanish | dia-LU-mo | Dialumo | *día* + *lumbre* felt. Reads as plausibly Spanish. |
| Italian | dia-LU-mo | Dialumo | *lume* (lamp, light) is heard. Good. |
| Portuguese | dʒia-LU-mu | Dialumo | final -o is reduced to -u. Fine. |
| French | dia-ly-MO | Dialumo | French *u* is /y/. *lumière* is still heard. |
| Japanese | | ディアルモ (di-a-ru-mo) | *aru mo* ("there is also"), harmless. Five morae. |
| Chinese | | 迪亚卢莫 (Dí-yà-lú-mò) | neutral characters. 莫 means "do not", a minor shade. |
| Hebrew | | דיאלומו | no meaning. |
| Arabic | | ديالومو | no meaning. |

- **Spelling from one hearing:** fair. Likely misspellings are *Dyalumo* and
  *Dialoomo*, and English listeners may write *Dialumo* correctly because of *dial*.
- **Bad or comic readings:** none serious. The antacid and aluminium echoes are
  remote.

**Rolls off the tongue: 3/5.** Four syllables, stress on the third:
di-a-LU-mo. The opening *dia* blurs into one syllable or two depending on the
speaker. It is pleasant once learned, but not effortless.

**Verdict: CLEAR.** It is the cleanest name on record. The weakness is sound,
not law.

## 2. Seralume: sera (Italian, evening) + lume (light)

**Trademarks: identical live mark**
- `"seralume"`: **1 result.** SERALUME, owned by **J. Miller Beauty Designs LLC**,
  serial **99695485**, **live/registered** on 29 Sep 2026. Class 3, beauty balm
  creams. https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=%22seralume%22
  - It is not in 9/41/42/44/45, but it is the **identical word**, registered last
    month, in self-care.
- `"seralumi"` 0, `"serolume"` 0.
- The root `LUME` is crowded: 35 marks.
  https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=%22lume%22
  - Lume Deodorant: class 3, two live marks.
  - LUME Management: class 44, health spa and IV drips.
  - Aleron: class 42, IT services.
  - Royal Caribbean: class 41, stage shows.
  - Sweetsense: class 9.

**Existing use**
- The web search found no page for Seralume. The engine "corrected" it to
  **salume** (Italian cured meat) and returned only salumi pages.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salumi
- No app found.

**Sound and meaning**
| language | said as | written | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | SER-uh-loom (3 syllables, silent e) | Seralume | **sera is the English plural of serum.** With a registered beauty balm, it reads as a skincare serum. Does not end on a vowel. |
| German | ze-ra-LU-me | Seralume | an initial S is voiced [z]. *Serum* is heard again. |
| Spanish | se-ra-LU-me | Seralume | *será* means "will be", so "será lume". Fine. |
| Italian | se-ra-LU-me | Seralume | real roots, "evening light". The best language for it, but it lands next to **salume**. |
| Portuguese | se-ra-LU-mi | Seralume | *será* again. Fine. |
| French | se-ra-LYM | Seralume | *sera* means "will be"; the final e is silent. |
| Japanese | | セラルーメ or セラリューム | セラ- is the prefix of serum (セラム). Cosmetic feel. |
| Chinese | | 赛拉卢美 (Sài-lā-lú-měi) | 美 (beauty) reinforces the cosmetic reading. |
| Hebrew | | סרלומה / סרלום | the spelling of the ending wavers. |
| Arabic | | سيرالوم | ending unclear. |

- **Spelling from one hearing:** poor. English listeners hear *Seraloom*,
  *Sera Loom* or *Serolume*.
- **Bad or comic readings:**
  - *serum*: in every Latin-script language and in Japanese.
  - *salume* (cured meat): the search engine's own correction.

**Rolls off the tongue: 3/5.** It is lovely in Italian (se-ra-LU-me), but in
English it collapses to three syllables with a silent e, which breaks the
Duolingo shape.

**Verdict: CONFLICT.** The identical word was registered in self-care on
29 Sep 2026, and the word itself already sounds like that product.

## 3. Sunela: sun + -ela

**Trademarks: only a dead mark**
- `"sunela"`: 1 result. SUNELA, owned by Abalioglu Yag (Turkey), serial
  79425584, class 29 (edible oils), **dead/abandoned** 6 Jul 2026.
  https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=%22sunela%22
- `"sunella"` 0, `"sunelle"` 0, `"sunila"` 0.

**Existing use**
- No exact use found. Near neighbours:
  - **SUNEL Group:** solar developer, Greece and London. https://sunelgroup.com/our-journey/
  - **Suniela:** beach cabanas. https://suniela.com/fr/pages/our-story
  - **Sisley Sunleÿa:** skincare.
- **It reads as a woman's name.**
  - It sits beside **Sunila**, a Hindu girl's name.
    https://hindutone.com/names/girls/s/girls-sunila/
  - It sits beside **Sunehla / Sunehri**, Hindi for "golden".
    https://www.ask-oracle.com/baby-name/sunehla/
  - **Sunel** is listed as a name meaning "lily".
    https://nameplay.org/name/Sunel
- This is a soft, mostly positive echo, but it means the name will feel like a
  person. No famous Sunela was found.
- No app found.

**Sound and meaning**
| language | said as | written | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | sun-EL-uh | Sunela | *sun* is heard, the whole point. Feminine name feel. |
| German | zu-NE-la | Sunela | an initial S is voiced [z] and the u is /u/, so *sun* is lost. |
| Spanish | su-NE-la | Sunela | *sun* is not a word here. It sounds like a girl's name or a town. |
| Italian | su-NE-la | Sunela | same as Spanish. Faint *suonerà*, harmless. |
| Portuguese | su-NE-la | Sunela | fine. |
| French | sy-ne-LA | Sunela | French *u* is /y/. Neutral. |
| Japanese | | スネラ (su-ne-ra) if read from the letters, サネラ if heard in English | **スネ (sune) means "shin"**, and it echoes スネ夫 (Suneo, the snob in Doraemon). Mild but real. |
| Chinese | | 苏内拉 (Sū-nèi-lā) | neutral. |
| Hebrew | | סונלה / סאנלה | the vowel depends on which pronunciation is chosen. |
| Arabic | | سونيلا / سانيلا | neutral. |

- **Spelling from one hearing:** good in English (*Sunella* is the risk). Weak
  elsewhere, because "soo-NEH-la" gets written *Sunela*, *Sounela* or *Zunela*.
- **The core problem:** **the "sun" root works only in English.** Ten of the
  eleven languages say *soo* and hear no sun.

**Rolls off the tongue: 4/5.** Three open syllables, stress on the middle:
su-NE-la. Easy everywhere. It is soft, but more of a name than a brand.

**Verdict: CLEAR on the record, with a CAUTION on meaning.** Nothing live was
found, and the logo already is a sun. But the meaning disappears outside
English, and in Japanese the sound is "shin".

  gives *Eliami*; English gives *Heliamy* or *Helyami*. The *ia* could be one
  syllable or two.

**Rolls off the tongue: 3/5.** Four syllables, stress on the third:

**Verdict: CAUTION.** Clear on trademarks, but the YouTube handle is taken, the

## Ranked summary

| rank | name | verdict | sound | key reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dialumo** | CLEAR | 3/5 | 0 US marks and no use anywhere. "Day + light" is heard in the Romance languages. It is four syllables, and English reads *dial-*. |
| 2 | **Sunela** | CLEAR / caution on meaning | 4/5 | Only a dead class 29 mark. Easy to say and fits the sun logo, but *sun* is heard only in English. It sounds like a girl's name, and スネ means "shin" in Japanese. |
| 3 | **Heliami** | CAUTION | 3/5 | 0 US marks, but @heliami is taken on YouTube. The silent h gives *Eliami* in the Romance languages, and ヘリ means "helicopter" in Japanese. |
| 4 | **Diami** | CAUTION | 5/5 | The best sound (*dime*, *d'ami*, *dā'imī* are all kind echoes), but DIAMI is live twice (jewellery, cosmetics) and diamonds own the search. |
| 5 | **Serami** | CONFLICT | 4/5 | SERAMI is live in **class 9** (earphones). It reads as "ceramic" almost everywhere and "salami" in Japanese and Chinese. |
| 6 | **Seralume** | CONFLICT | 3/5 | SERALUME was registered 29 Sep 2026 for a beauty balm. *Sera* is the plural of serum, and the search engine reads it as *salume*. |

**Bottom line:** none of the six both sounds like Duolingo and is free.
- The two that are free on the record (Dialumo, Sunela) each pay in sound or meaning.
- The one that sounds best (Diami) is already held as a word.
- If one name goes forward, **Dialumo** is the safest and **Sunela** the most
  sayable. Either needs the EU, UK, WIPO and J-PlatPat searches that this
  session could not reach, done by hand before anything is filed.
