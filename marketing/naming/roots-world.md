# World roots for an invented name

Brief: an evening-reflection journal with a small-sun logo. Themes: body and mind, the day, light and the sun, reflection, presence, calm, wholeness, memory, becoming.
Languages: Polynesian (Hawaiian, Māori, Samoan), Swahili, Yoruba, Zulu/Xhosa, Amharic, Persian, Turkish, Arabic, Hebrew, Finnish, Icelandic, Norwegian/Danish/Swedish, Welsh/Irish.
Researched 2026-10-24.

**How to read the table**
- **Rating (1-5)** says how well the root works as a *building block* that people anywhere can say. It rewards short open syllables (CV-CV), no tones, no clusters, no special letters, no clash in EN/ES/PT/JA/DE, and room left to own it. It does not rate how nice the word is.
- **Slang/clash** results come from my own knowledge of EN/ES/PT/JA/DE. The one exception is *nuru*, which I looked up. Have a native speaker confirm before you shortlist.
- **Brand crowding** results come from the searches cited plus general knowledge. **None of this is a trademark search.** Run a USPTO/EUIPO/WIPO and App Store search on any finalist.
- Where a source link is a lookup page rather than a cited fact, the row says so.

## Do not build on these (sacred, protected or saturated)

| Root | Why |
|---|---|
| **mana** (Hawaiian/Māori) | Sacred power/authority. IPONZ advises against commercialising tapu concepts. It is also saturated in games and wellness. [IPONZ/Wai262 coverage](https://stoppress.co.nz/?p=6527) |
| **aloha** | Native Hawaiian groups have actively defended it. The Aloha Poke trademark fight drew condemnation from the Office of Hawaiian Affairs and a boycott. [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/asian-america/native-hawaiian-activists-call-boycott-protest-over-aloha-poke-trademark-n898691), [HPR](https://www.hawaiipublicradio.org/local-news/2018-08-02/can-anyone-own-aloha) |
| **aroha, mauri, wairua** (Māori) | Love, life force, spirit. Brand use is publicly contested, as in the Aroha/Vyra case and HOKA. [The Spinoff](https://thespinoff.co.nz/atea/12-02-2026/from-aroha-to-vyra-the-brand-that-sparked-a-debate-on-cultural-appropriation), [Taiuru](https://www.taiuru.maori.nz/cultural-appropriation-questions/) |
| **pono** (Hawaiian) | A core moral value ("righteousness, balance"). Branding it reads as appropriation. [Pono (word)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pono_(word)) |
| **sakina** (Arabic) | The Qur'anic divine tranquility "sent down into hearts" (48:4). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakina), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/2026/02/20/learn-arabic-sakina-tranquillity/) |
| **nefesh** (Hebrew) | Soul, in a strongly scriptural register. It is also the name of the large org Nefesh B'Nefesh. |
| **yawm** (Arabic) | Day. Hard to say (aw + m), and it reads as EN "yawn". |
| **hygge / sisu / lagom** | Saturated wellness buzzwords. Hygge and Sisu already have wellness apps; sisu also means grit, not calm. [Sisu app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sisu/id6758745440), [Hygge app](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6751844488), [Bloomberg on sisu](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2018-04-27/finland-s-sisu-is-your-new-nordic-life-improvement-buzzword), [Lagom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagom) |
| **hwyl** (Welsh) | A lovely meaning (fervour, spirit, "goodbye") that nobody outside Wales can pronounce. [WeLearnWelsh](https://welearnwelsh.com/words/welsh-word-hwyl-fun-goodbye-sail/) |
| **shams** (Arabic, sun) | EN "shams" = fakes. |

## Eight suggested blends

Not trademark-checked. Each is built from roots rated 3 or higher.

| Blend | Built from | Reads as | Say it | Why it might work / watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Solva** | sól (sun) + shal**va** (tranquility) | "calm sun" | SOL-vah | Two syllables, readable everywhere, and *sol* is understood in ES/PT/IT. Solva is a Welsh village and close to Solvay (chemicals). |
| **Valia** | **val**o (light) + **lā** (day/sun) | "light of the day" | VAH-lee-ah | Soft, open vowels, works in JA (ヴァリア). It is an existing first name, so it is hard to own alone. |
| **Laora** | **lā** (day) + **ora** (light) | "day-light" | lah-OR-ah | Two light roots. IT *ora* = "now" adds *presence*. Check against Oura for confusion. |
| **Jualo** | **jua** (sun; to know) + va**lo** (light) | "sun-light / knowing" | JOO-ah-lo | The Swahili double meaning (sun / to know yourself) suits reflection. ES readers say "HOO-a-lo". |
| **Solie** | **sól** (sun) + mā**lie** (calm) | "calm sun" | SO-lee | Very easy to say. It borrows only the tail of *mālie*, which keeps clear of Malie Organics, but confirm. |
| **Hugola** | **hug**ur (mind) + **ola** (life) | "mind-life" | HOO-go-la / HUG-o-la | The body–mind theme, with a warm EN "hug" inside. Slightly playful. |
| **Solanga** | **sól** + i**langa** (both "sun") | "sun, twice" | so-LAN-ga | Pairs North and South (Iceland and Southern Africa), with a strong rhythm. Doubling the sun suits a sun logo. |
| **Grianola** | **grian** (sun) + **ola** (life) | "sun-life", and "granola" | gree-an-OH-la | Wildcard. A food pun fits the app's nutrition roots, but it may read as a joke or a cereal. |
