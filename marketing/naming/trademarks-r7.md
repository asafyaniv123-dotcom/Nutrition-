# Name screen, round 7: seven names built on "Sol"

Screened 24 Oct 2026. This is not legal advice and not a clearance search.

Candidates: Sol Days, Dear Sol, Little Sol, Solecito, Sol and Me, Hola Sol, Solday.
Store title in every case: "<Name>: Daily Reflection".

## What was searched, and what could not be

- **US trademarks:** Trademarkia. Each name was searched three ways:
  as one joined word (`?q=solday`), as loose words (`?q=sol%20days`), and as
  a **quoted phrase** (`?q=%22sol%20days%22`).
  - The loose two-word search is useless for this round: it ranks on the common
    word, so "sol days" returns 7 DAYS / DAYS and "little sol" returns LITTLE A,
    out of "30,000" hits. It was run and read, but proves nothing.
  - The quoted search was **calibrated** on a known mark: `"sunny me"` returns
    the live SUNNY ME (99498296) found in round 3. So a zero from a quoted search
    is a real signal, stronger than in earlier rounds.
  - Bare `SOL` (3,284 results) was read for its first three pages, which is where
    the two wellness-app SOL marks below surfaced. **Pages 4-329 were not read.**
- **Not reachable from this session:**
  - EUIPO TMview (`tmdn.org/tmview/api/...`): connection reset (ECONNRESET), as in every earlier round.
  - EUIPO eSearch (`euipo.europa.eu/eSearch`): connection reset.
  - WIPO Global Brand Database (`branddb.wipo.int`): ALTCHA bot-check page, no results.
  - USPTO TSDR (`tsdr.uspto.gov`): HTTP 429, so truncated goods texts could not be completed.
  - **So EU, UK, WIPO/Madrid, Japan and China are NOT covered.** Before filing,
    run the shortlist through TMview, the WIPO Global Brand Database and J-PlatPat by hand.
- **Apps, companies, books, songs, podcasts:** general web search, plus searches
  restricted to apps.apple.com and play.google.com. The search engine indexes
  the stores thinly, so "no app found" is weak evidence.

Verdicts: **CLEAR** nothing relevant found / **CAUTION** something similar, or
something that owns the search results, explained / **CONFLICT** the same or
dominant name is registered or used in software or wellness.

## General: "Sol" itself

### SOL is registered for a wellness app, and that app is the closest thing on the store

**"Sol - Self Care & Community"** (Sol Technologies, Inc., Miami / Lewes DE),
Health & Fitness, 4.9 from 1.3K ratings, subtitle *Build habits, support, purpose*.
https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sol-find-purpose-community/id1474958416

What it does, from its own listing: 4-week plans, daily guided practices,
**"mood tracker, mood journal, daily journal, and gratitude journal"**,
astrology and personality quizzes, community Circles, matched groups called
**"Solmates"**, and **an AI companion named "Sol"**. The same app id has carried

`sol-journal-habit-community`, `sol-daily-goodness`, `sol-spiritual-wellness-daily`,
`sol-inner-growth-community`, `sol-grow-your-inner-life`, `sol-live-with-purpose`.
So it has at different times called itself a **journal**, **daily**, and **habit** app.

**Consequence for every name below:** in each one "Sol" is the distinctive
part and the other word (Days, Dear, Little, and Me, Hola, -day) is ordinary.
That is the shape in which a registered SOL is most likely to be asserted.
And two of the candidates (Dear Sol, Sol and Me) address a character called
Sol, which is exactly what Sol Technologies' AI companion is.

### English associations

- **S.O.L. = "shit out of luck."** Listed by Wiktionary as the English
  adjective sense of SOL. https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/SOL . It reads that way
  mainly in capitals ("SOL"), so the wordmark should never be set in all caps.
- **SOL = Solana's ticker.** Wiktionary: "unofficial, non-ISO 4217 currency code
  for the cryptocurrency Solana". It dominates finance search results for "SOL"
  (CoinDesk, TradingView, DEX pages). A search for "sol app" will surface wallets.
- Other English uses, all harmless: statute of limitations, Standards of
  Learning (Virginia school tests), slice of life; **a sol is a Martian day**
  (NASA usage), which gives "Sol Days" and "Solday" an unintended Mars reading;
  sol is the 5th note of the scale (do-re-mi-fa-**sol**); the Peruvian currency.
  https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/sol
- Nothing else offensive was found.

### Other languages (from Wiktionary unless marked)

- **Spanish, Portuguese:** sun. Also the note G, and Peru's currency. Natural.
- **Italian:** the note G ("sol"); **also an apocopic (shortened) form of "sole",
  sun**, so an Italian reads it as sun, slightly poetic. No problem.
- **French:** **sol = ground, floor, soil** (and the note G). A French reader's
  first reading is "the ground", not the sun. "Sol Days" = days of the floor;
  "Petit sol" would be wrong. Mildly odd, not negative.
- **German:** "Sol" is the chemistry colloid and the astronomical name of the
  sun; not everyday. Close to *Soll* (target, quota; debit in bookkeeping), a
  faint "obligation" echo. Not negative.
- **Danish, Norwegian, Swedish:** sun, and figuratively a source of joy. Good.
- **Hebrew:** סול is the musical note sol (and the Ladino word for sun). No
  negative meaning found. Sounds close to סל (basket); harmless.
- **Japanese:** ソル (soru) is the note sol; no other meaning found. Romanised
  Latin names display fine.
- **Chinese:** no fixed rendering. 索尔 is used for the Peruvian sol and is also
  how Marvel's *Thor* is written (雷神索尔) — not negative, but a transliteration
  should be chosen deliberately rather than inherited.
- **Arabic:** سُول is listed as the musical note. Unvoweled, the same letters can
  be read as the verb سوّل ("to make something seem attractive, to entice",
  Quranic, negative register) or سُؤْل-adjacent "wish". Not verified with a
  native speaker; low risk because the store name will be written in Latin.
- **Polish *sól*, Serbo-Croatian/Slovene *sol*:** salt. **Romanian:** floor/ground.
  **Turkish, Azerbaijani:** "left". None is in the 11 shipped languages.
