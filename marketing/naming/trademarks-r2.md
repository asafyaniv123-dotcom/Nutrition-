# Name screen, round 2: twelve two-word names

Screened 24 Oct 2026. This is not legal advice and not a clearance search.

## What was searched, and what could not be

- **US trademarks:** Trademarkia, searching each name both as two words
  (`https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=whole%20me`) and as one word
  (`?q=wholeme`). Every hit worth reporting was opened on its own record page.
  **Caveat:** a two-word search returns a ranked list and only the first page
  (10 results) is readable. A clean one-word search, which matches the exact
  joined string, is the stronger "nothing found" signal.
- **Not reachable from this session:**
  - EUIPO TMview (`tmdn.org/tmview` API): connection reset (ECONNRESET), as in round 1.
  - WIPO Global Brand Database (`branddb.wipo.int`): returns a CAPTCHA page, no results.
  - trademarks.justia.com and uspto.report: HTTP 403 in round 1, so not retried.
  - **So EU, UK, WIPO/Madrid, Japan and China are NOT covered.** Before filing,
    run the shortlist through TMview, WIPO Global Brand Database and J-PlatPat by hand.
- **Existing uses:** general web search, plus searches restricted to
  apps.apple.com and play.google.com. The search engine indexes the stores
  thinly, so "no app found" is weak evidence. Search both stores directly too.

Verdicts: **CLEAR**: nothing found that matters / **CAUTION**: something
similar, explained / **CONFLICT**: the same or near-same name already in
software, wellness or self-help.

## Brighter Me

Trademarks:
- No mark "BRIGHTER ME" or "BRIGHTERME" was found. The one-word search returns
  **0 results**: https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=brighterme
- BRIGHTER alone is live in health: **CIGNA**, serials 85121117 and 87221870
  (classes 35 and 36, dental and health care referral); **Novartis AG**, serial
  79429832 (classes 38 and 41, online forums for healthcare professionals);
  BRIGHTERSIDE, serial 98576058 (class 44, home health care); BE BRIGHTER,
  serial 79352147 (classes 3 and 5).
  https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=brighter%20me

Existing uses: no app, book or podcast named "Brighter Me" or "BrightMe" was
found. A web search for "Brighter Me" wellness apps returns **BetterMe**
results instead, which is itself a sign of how close the two read.
Podcasts nearby: *The Brighter Podcast* (https://castbox.fm/channel/6436216),
*Live Brighter With Tara* (https://rephonic.com/podcasts/live-brighter-with-tara),
*Chasing Brighter* (https://chasingbrighter.podbean.com/page/4/).

**Similarity to BetterMe** (search: https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=betterme):
- BetterMe Limited holds live US registrations for BETTERME in **class 9**
  (serial 97185291, with 25), classes 5 and 35 (98191146) and 25 (97683772),
  and has a pending application in classes 27 and 28 (99114763). BETTERME
  BUSINESS HOLDINGS CORP. holds class 41 (87733720). BetterMe runs fitness and
  mental-health apps (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1363010081,
  https://betterme.world/about).
- A likelihood-of-confusion test compares sight, sound, meaning and commercial
  impression. "Brighter Me" and "BetterMe" share the same structure (comparative
  adjective + "Me"), the same first letter, the same "-ter Me" ending and the
  same meaning (an improved self). They would also be sold as the same kind of
  goods (wellness apps) in the same channels (the app stores). Every factor
  points the same way.
- The rename exists *because* of BetterMe. A name that keeps the pattern
  carries the old risk with it. It could also look like an attempt to stay
  close to a larger brand, which is the pattern an examiner or BetterMe's
  lawyers would be watching for.

**Verdict: CONFLICT.** The exact name is unregistered, but it is the closest
of the twelve to BetterMe's registered class 9 mark, in the same market. A
rename away from BetterMe should not land here.
