# Name screen, round 12b: nine plain-English "light" names

Screened 24 Oct 2026. This is not legal advice and not a clearance search.

Candidates: Your Light, Own Light, Light Within, Shine Within, Light the Day,
Keep the Light, Carry the Light, Kindly Lit, Glow Today.
Store title in every case: "<Name>: Daily Reflection".

## What was searched, and what could not be

- **US trademarks:** Trademarkia, each name as a **quoted phrase**
  (`?q=%22your%20light%22`) and as **one joined word** (`?q=yourlight`).
  The loose two-word search was not used; it ranks on the common word and proves nothing.
  - The quoted search is **calibrated**: it returned the known marks YOUR LIGHT,
    KEEP THE LIGHT, CARRY THE LIGHT, THE LIGHT WITHIN, SHINE YOUR LIGHT and FIND YOUR LIGHT.
    So its zeros are real zeros for the *exact* phrase.
  - It is an **exact-mark** match: `"light within"` returned 0 while
    `"the light within"` returned 2. Longer marks *containing* a candidate are
    therefore not covered except where a variant was searched by hand
    (THE LIGHT WITHIN, YOUR OWN LIGHT, FIND YOUR LIGHT, SHINE YOUR LIGHT).
  - Bare `GLOW` and bare `SHINE` were read only for page 1 of the class-9 filter
    (GLOW: 141 results; SHINE: 151, of which 21 in class 9). **The rest was not read.**
  - Classes 9/41/42/44/45 are reported where a hit exists; goods texts on
    Trademarkia's list view are truncated.
- **Not reachable from this session (same as earlier rounds):**
  - EUIPO TMview (`tmdn.org/tmview/api/...`): ECONNRESET.
  - EUIPO eSearch (`euipo.europa.eu/eSearch`): ECONNRESET.
  - WIPO Global Brand Database (`branddb.wipo.int`): ALTCHA bot-check page, no results.
  - **So EU, UK, WIPO/Madrid, Japan and China are NOT covered.** Run the
    shortlist through TMview, WIPO Global Brand Database and J-PlatPat by hand before filing.
- **Apps, companies, books, songs, podcasts:** general web search, plus searches
  restricted to apps.apple.com and play.google.com. The search engine indexes
  the stores thinly, so "no app found" is weak evidence.

Verdicts: **CLEAR** nothing relevant found / **CAUTION** something similar,
a religious or product reading, explained / **CONFLICT** the same or dominant
name is registered or used in software, wellness or journaling.

  https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=%22your%20light%22

- Neighbours in the same phrase family:

    charitable fundraising) and 86729371 (class 35, awareness of sexual assault and violence).
    A dead SHINE YOUR LIGHT (85962693, Wellness Within You LLC) was in **class 45,

    https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=%22shine%20your%20light%22
  - FIND YOUR LIGHT, Lamps Plus, Inc., 99652928, **live/pending**, classes 11 and 35
    (lighting retail). A FIND YOUR LIGHT in class 9 (Ostendo, 97220373) is dead.
    https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=%22find%20your%20light%22

**Apps, companies, works**
- No app titled "Your Light" found. One iOS app, *Seize Light* (a camera light meter),
  has a feature card called "Your Light Score":
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/seize-light/id6784576883
- Search results are owned by Christian content: the LDS "Light the World" campaign
  slogan *"The World Needs Your Light"* (https://www.deseretbook.com/product/6016690.html),
  *Send Out Your Light* (Sandra McCracken, scripture and song),
  and parish podcast episodes.

**Meaning**
- Strongly scriptural in English: *"Let your light so shine before men"* (Matthew 5:16),
  *"O send out thy light"* (Psalm 43:3). Not exclusively religious, but the
  religious reading is the first one in search.
- Very low distinctiveness: two common words, hard to own and hard to find in a store.

**Verdict: CAUTION** - no software or wellness mark found, but the phrase is
weak, crowded (SHINE/FIND YOUR LIGHT are live in other classes) and its
dominant reading online is Christian.

## 8. Kindly Lit

**Trademarks**
- `"kindly lit"`: **0.** `kindlylit`: **0.**
  https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=%22kindly%20lit%22

**Apps, companies, works**
- **Nothing** found under the name: no app, company, book, song or podcast. Search
  returned only a French children's bed line "KIDLY" and dictionary pages.

**Meaning**
- Echoes **"Lead, Kindly Light"**, John Henry Newman's 1833 hymn - one of the best-known
  hymns in English, sung at funerals and memorials.
  https://hymnary.org/hymn/GH1899/1 ,
  https://www.goodreads.com/topic/list_book/52770818
- **"Lit" is English slang for drunk or high** (and for "exciting"); "kindly lit" can
  read as "pleasantly drunk" - an awkward reading for a wellness app.
- "Lit" also abbreviates *literature*; next to "Kindly" it can suggest Amazon **Kindle**
  to some readers (no Kindle-formative mark was searched).

**Verdict: CAUTION** - legally empty, but the slang reading of "lit" and the
memorial-hymn echo are both real for an English-speaking audience.
