# Store listing — English (the original every other language is written from)

The audience is everyone: every age, every walk of life. So the listing
doesn't speak to a persona. It makes one promise any person recognises, and
then opens several doors into it.

**Description** (4000)

Most days disappear. Olimei keeps them.

Every evening, Olimei asks you a few questions about your day. Not
generic prompts: questions about the day you actually lived, drawn from what
you noted along the way. The lunch you had, the workout you did, the friend
you called. One question at a time, never a form. Answer what you like, skip
what you don't, stop whenever you want and pick up later.

Week after week, those evenings add up to something no tracker gives you: a
picture of your life.

FOR EVERY KIND OF DAY
• You've tried keeping a diary and the blank page won. Here you never start
  from nothing.
• You already track food or workouts and want more than numbers.
• You just want to remember your days, and see how you are actually doing.

DAY'S END
The heart of the app. A calm look back at today, a few minutes before sleep.
A short summary first; a deeper pass when you want one.

EVERYTHING FEEDS THE EVENING
• Nutrition: log meals in your own language, from a food library built for
  eleven of them.
• Fitness: workouts, sets and progress over time.
• Closet: photograph your clothes and note what you wore.
• People: the people who matter, and when you last spoke.
• Time: plan the day, hour by hour.
• Journal: everything you've written, day by day.

YOUR WEEK, IN ONE SENTENCE
At the end of each week, a short review of how it went, closing with one
sentence to carry into the next.

MEET BEEM
A small sun who keeps you company in the evenings and celebrates the days you
show up.

YOURS
Your journal stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up.

ELEVEN LANGUAGES
English, Español, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Português, 日本語, 简体中文,
繁體中文, العربية and עברית, each written for its own readers, not
machine-translated.
