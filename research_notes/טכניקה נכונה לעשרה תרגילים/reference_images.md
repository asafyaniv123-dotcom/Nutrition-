# Free-licence pose-reference images for ten exercises

Method: all candidates were found on Wikimedia Commons with the API (`generator=search` and `generator=categorymembers`, `prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|extmetadata`). Every licence and author below was read from the file's own `extmetadata` fields (`LicenseShortName`, `LicenseUrl`, `Artist`, `Credit`, `AttributionRequired`), not from a search snippet. Form was judged by eye from 420 px and 900 px renders of each candidate. Judging form from a still photo has limits, and those limits are noted where they apply.

The licence types that turn up below:
- **PD (US gov)**: a work of a US federal employee, public domain. No attribution is legally required, though a courtesy credit line is given.
- **CC BY / CC BY-SA**: attribution is required (`AttributionRequired=true` in the metadata). For CC BY-SA, a *redrawing* in a different style that copies only the pose is very likely not a derivative work. If the drawings trace or closely follow the source, treat them as derivatives: BY-SA would then require the drawings to be released under BY-SA too. Prefer PD sources where possible.
- **Everkinetic**: a CC BY-SA 3.0 line-drawing series. Credit line: "Everkinetic (everkinetic.com), CC BY-SA 3.0".

## For each exercise: correct-form free images (URL, licence, credit, angle, phase, form verdict)

### Takeaway
Usable free references were found for all ten exercises. Weak spots:
- **Pull-up**: no side view.
- **Bodyweight lunge**: only dumbbell-loaded drawings.
- **Glute bridge**: few options.
- **Bent-over row**: the only overhand barbell row photo is a single mid-pull frame.

For the **bench-press grip**, the best image is the public-domain US Army close-up *Bench Press (4517332).jpg*. It shows both hands fully wrapped (thumbs round the bar) with the bar low in the palm.

### Inferences
- **Most consistent single source:** Everkinetic (CC BY-SA 3.0). It covers push-up, lunge, pull-up, row posture and glute bridge in one line-art style with start and end frames. Squat via the `Squats.svg` derivative.
- **Public-domain photos:** US military images (DVIDS photos mirrored on Commons) are the best PD material for bench-press grip, overhead press, plank and squat bottom. They carry no share-alike, but they are rarely true side views.
- **Weakest sources:** the overhead press and the overhand bent-over row have no clean side-view free image. A redraw will have to infer the side profile, or work from a frame of the CC BY 3.0 FitnessScape videos. Those videos are `Bent-over row - exercise demonstration video.webm` and `Shoulder press - exercise demonstration video.webm`; they were found but not inspected.

### Gaps
- **Sources not searched:** NIH/NIA Go4Life, US Army FM 7-22 / H2F illustrations and armypubs.army.mil were not checked within the tool budget. Only Wikimedia Commons, including its DVIDS mirrors, was searched.
- **Bench grip details:** no image was found that shows forearms truly vertical and straight wrists from a clean side angle at the same time. The 4517332 close-up shows the wrap and the bar position in the palm. Forearm verticality has to come from the Eisman side view.
- **Bodyweight squat:** only the first frame of `Bodyweight Squats.gif` was checked, so its bottom position is unverified.
- **Side views missing:** standing barbell overhead press and pull-up.
- **Lunge:** no free *bodyweight* forward-lunge image with verified form was found. The references hold dumbbells.
- **Form verdicts:** made by eye from stills. Subtle faults such as lumbar flexion under load or knee valgus cannot be ruled out from a single frame.
