# Free-licence pose-reference images for six more exercises

Method: every candidate was found on Wikimedia Commons with the API (`generator=search`, quoted-phrase searches such as `"lat pull-down"` / `"leg press"` / `"shoulder press"`, and `generator=categorymembers` on *Leg press machines*, *Pulldown machines*, *Biceps curls*, *Overhead press*). Every licence and author below was read from the file's own `extmetadata` (`LicenseShortName`, `LicenseUrl`, `Artist`, `Credit`, `AttributionRequired`, `UsageTerms`), not from a search snippet. Form was judged by eye from 420 px renders of each candidate on contact sheets (about 75 files looked at). Judging form from a still has limits, noted where they apply.

Licence types below:
- **PD (US gov)**: work of a US federal employee (DVIDS / USMC / CDC); `AttributionRequired=false`. A courtesy credit is given.
- **CC0**: no attribution required.
- **CC BY / CC BY-SA**: attribution required (`AttributionRequired=true`). A redrawing in a different style that copies only the pose is very likely not a derivative; if a drawing traces the source, BY-SA would bind the drawing to BY-SA. Prefer PD/CC0 where it exists.
- **Everkinetic** credit line: "Everkinetic (everkinetic.com), CC BY-SA 3.0".
- **George Stepanek series** (model "Emily", same gym, each file shows start and finish side by side): metadata says "No machine-readable author provided. GeorgeStepanek assumed (based on copyright claims)" / "Own work assumed". Credit line: "George Stepanek, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0".

## For each exercise: correct-form free images showing pose and equipment (URL, licence, credit, angle, phase, verdict)

### Takeaway
Usable references exist for all six, but **public-domain photos that show both correct form and the machine from the side are scarce**: there is no PD side view of a lat pulldown, an RDL, a dumbbell curl or a dumbbell shoulder press. The best side views are the George Stepanek photo pair (pulldown, pushdown, leg press; CC BY-SA 3.0) and Everkinetic line art (CC BY-SA 3.0). PD/CC0 fills in the machine itself (pulldown station, 45-degree sled) and front views. **Two Everkinetic sets must not be used**: the "wide grip lat pull down" drawings show a **behind-the-neck** pull.

### Inferences
- **Most consistent single source:** Everkinetic. It covers all six exercises in one line-art style with start and turnaround frames: RDL, curl, pushdown, leg press, shoulder press, and, **for the pulldown, only the rejected behind-the-neck set**.
- **Pulldown, pushdown and leg press machines:** the George Stepanek photo set (one model, one gym, start and finish side by side, CC BY-SA 3.0) gives the most useful side views of the machine with correct form. They are low-resolution.
- **PD material** (DVIDS, USMC, CDC) is good for the machines themselves (Touchpoint pulldown station, Drawsko sled, USMC cable stack) and for front views. None of it is a clean side view of correct form for these six.
- **A redraw that needs the side profile of the lat pulldown or the shoulder press** must infer it: the pulldown from the rear-3/4 Stepanek frames, the press from front/rear views. Or it could work from a frame of the CC BY 3.0 videos on Commons, which were found but not inspected: `Hip Sled - How to perform a 45 degree leg press.webm` (FitnessScape), `Shoulder press - exercise demonstration video.webm` (FitnessScape), `How To Properly Dumbbell Shoulder Press.webm` (Colossus Fitness), `Common Lat Pulldown Mistakes.webm` (Andrew Kwong). The CDC's `Muscle Strengthening at the Gym - Seated Leg Press.webm` is PD but shows a horizontal seated press, not a 45-degree sled.

### Gaps
- **Missing side views:** no PD/CC0 side view with correct form was found for the lat pulldown, RDL, standing dumbbell curl or dumbbell shoulder press.
- **Sources not searched:** only Wikimedia Commons, including its DVIDS mirrors, was searched. DVIDS directly, CDC/NIA print materials and armypubs (H2F) were not checked within the tool budget.
- **Videos not inspected:** the CC BY 3.0 demonstration videos above were not opened, so their form is unverified.
- **Leg press bottom:** the Everkinetic -1 frame is the only verified bottom position. Whether the hips stay down at depth cannot be confirmed from a photograph here, because the only PD bottom shot (Drawsko) shows a questionable knees-hugged position.
- **Form verdicts:** made by eye from stills of 420 px or less. Subtle faults (lumbar rounding at the RDL turnaround, slight elbow drift) cannot be ruled out from one frame.
