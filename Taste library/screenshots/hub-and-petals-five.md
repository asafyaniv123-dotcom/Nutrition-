# hub-and-petals-five.jpg

Five white petals fanned around a central disc that says "5 OPTIONS".
Each petal carries an icon, a number (01-05), a heading and a paragraph, and
each is joined to the hub by a coloured spine in its own colour - red, teal,
green, yellow, orange. Pale lavender-grey ground, soft shadows.

## What to notice

**The spines are the whole thing.** Every petal is physically CONNECTED
to the centre by a coloured bar that runs into the hub. That connection is what
makes five separate shapes read as one system rather than as five cards that
happen to be near each other.

**The colour lives on the spine, not the fill.** Each petal is white. Its colour
appears only on the connector, the number and the icon — so five colours never
become a rainbow.

**The hub names the whole.** "5 OPTIONS" tells you what you are looking at
before you read any of it.

## Where it applies

The home board. Our nine areas float on the board unconnected, all
the same cream, in no order. The hub already exists — סיום יום — but nothing
runs between it and the areas, and the memory says the reflection is the spine
that the other areas feed. The diagram draws exactly that relationship and our
screen does not.

## The hard part

Five petals at this size carry a paragraph each. We have NINE areas and a
phone. Body text per area is not available to us, and colour on nine spokes is
a rainbow unless the palette is deliberately narrow.
