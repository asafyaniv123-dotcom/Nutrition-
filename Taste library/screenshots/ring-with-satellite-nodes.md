# ring-with-satellite-nodes.jpg

A grey ring with a white disc naming the whole, and eight circular
nodes around it — four left, four right — each with an icon and a coloured arc
around its rim, joined to the hub by thin right-angled leader lines.

Sent 16 September 2026 as one of four, all for the home screen.

## What to notice

**Eight is the closest of the four to our nine.** It shows the count
we actually have, and it does not fan them — it stacks them in two columns and
connects them with lines.

**The colour is a partial arc on each node's rim.** Not a fill, not a spine: a
stroke on part of the circle. Cheap, quiet, and it gives each node an identity
without colouring the node.

**The connectors are right angles, not radial spokes.** Which is what lets the
nodes sit in tidy columns instead of on a circle — a layout that suits a tall
phone far better than a wheel does.

## Where it applies

If the arc is ever abandoned, this is the shape to go to: two columns
of areas, an arc of colour on each, thin connectors to a hub. It is the only one
of the four that would survive being nine items on a 390px screen.

## The hard part

It is also the least like what we have, so it is the most expensive to
try — and the arc layout was measured and tuned (chord = 2R sin(step/2)).
