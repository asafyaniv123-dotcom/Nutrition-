# neumorphic-fan-with-gauge.jpg

Five white rounded-trapezoid tiles fanned in an arc above a central
circle. Between them and the hub sits a thin dark arc with small coloured dots
along it, like a gauge. Below, three numbered badges with gradient rings.

Sent 16 September 2026 as one of four, all for the home screen.

## What to notice

**The arc between the fan and the hub does two jobs at once.** It
separates them and it measures something — the dots sit at positions along it.
A ring that is both a boundary and a scale.

**The tiles are trapezoids, not rectangles.** They are cut to the arc they sit
on, so the fan reads as one object rather than five tiles rotated.

**The numbered badges below are a different density.** Big number, ring, short
title, one line — which is what survives on a phone where the fan's tiles do
not.

## Where it applies

The arc is already ours — the nine areas sit on one. What is missing
is anything that reads as a MEASURE. A ring around the סיום יום circle that
fills with the day would turn decoration into information.

## The hard part

Our capsules are upright pills, not trapezoids cut to the arc, and
cutting them to it would fight Hebrew's vertical text.
