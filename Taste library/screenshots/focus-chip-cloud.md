# focus-chip-cloud.png

A goal picker from a wellness app: "What do you want to focus on? Pick up to 3."
A centred cloud of pill chips, each a small rendered 3D icon plus a label,
rows of two staggered so the cloud has an uneven, hand-set edge. A step number
and "Set goals" at the top, back on one side, Skip on the other; a soft
Continue that stays disabled until something is picked.

## What Asaf said

> זאת דוגמה למשהו שראיתי שאולי יכול לעבוד היות ויש לנו הרבה אזורים.

Sent 21 Oct 2026 for step 3 of the first run ("what matters to you").

## What was taken

The cloud and the rendered icon per chip. Our seven areas got one object each in
the style of the existing Beem props (plate, dumbbell, heart, and four new ones:
palette, bulb, calendar, shirt) - `review/first-3/props/`. A chosen chip is
pressed in with a soft light of the area's colour, the app's own grammar,
rather than the reference's outline.
