# a-room-inside-the-key.png

A cream keyboard, rendered in soft 3D. Where a block of keys would be, the
surface has been hollowed into a tiny bedroom: a bed with a figure asleep in a
pink eye-mask, a framed leaf print on the wall, a nightstand with an alarm
clock and a small lit lamp, slippers on the floor. The key below the hollow
reads "home".

## What to notice

**THE ROOM IS CARVED OUT OF THE INTERFACE'S OWN MATERIAL.** It is not a sticker
placed on the keyboard and not a window onto somewhere else — it is the same
cream, the same matte surface, the same light, with a hollow in it. That is why
it reads as the keyboard being alive rather than as a picture sitting on top of
one. It is also exactly the gesture this app already owns: everything here is
one material with things pressed into it.

**THE ONLY COLOUR IN THE ENTIRE IMAGE IS THE LAMP.** Cream on cream on cream,
and one warm light. That is [[soft-white-light-inside]] as a photograph — "one
material, and the only colour is light in a pressed channel". Of everything in
this folder, this is the closest thing to a picture of our own rule.

**THE STATE IS "NOT YET".** The character is asleep, the lamp is on, the clock
is there. Nothing is happening and the scene is still full of anticipation,
which is precisely what he said he wants: something waiting for you. A sleeping
character says "come back" far better than a waving one.

**AND IT IS A BUTTON.** The hollow sits in a keyboard, directly above a key.
His own words: a button it comes out of.

## Where it applies

**The hub on the home screen** is the obvious candidate — the disc in the
middle of the fan, which is a button, which is pressed into the material
already, and which is where the sun was drawn and then removed. A niche in it
that the sun sleeps in is the same move as this image, in our vocabulary.

**And it gives the "visits" idea a home.** TODO already carries the rule that
the character should appear at four moments at most, because a growing list is
wallpaper-in-waiting. A sleeping place is the opposite problem solved: the
character is permanently present and almost never performing.

## The hard part

**A diorama is not a flat UI.** This is an isometric 3D scene with furniture.
What transfers is the HOLLOW and the one warm light; what does not transfer is
the bedroom. The honest translation is a pressed alcove in the hub with the sun
curled in it and a single lit edge — not a room with objects in it.

**A SLEEPING SUN IS A DARK SUN**, and the sun is this app's light. That is
either the best thing about the idea or a contradiction, and it has to be
decided rather than discovered: either the sun sleeps until the day is closed
and waking is the reward, or it is awake all day and sleeps once you have
finished — which would make sleep the reward instead. Those are opposite
products.

**And the home screen is not freely ours.** The fan, the rays and the hub were
settled over several rounds and the current centre is a plain button at his own
request ([[home-screen-direction]]). Anything that puts a creature back in the
middle of that screen is re-opening a decision he already closed, so it is
shown in `dev/look` before it is built anywhere.
