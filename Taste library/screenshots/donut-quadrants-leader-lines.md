# donut-quadrants-leader-lines.jpg

A donut split into four quadrants of one blue family, a white disc in
the middle naming the whole, an icon on each quadrant, and a thin leader line
dropping from each to a labelled column of text underneath.

## What Asaf said

> זה נחמד. אבל חשבתי פשוט על עוד הרבה רעיונות. קח דוגמה: כל אלה אפשרויות נוספות למסך הבית.

Sent 16 September 2026 as one of four, all for the home screen.

## What to notice

**One hue, four values.** All four quadrants are blue — light to
near-black. It is unmistakably four things and it is not a rainbow, which is
the answer to the problem the five-colour version creates.

**The leader lines put the reading somewhere else.** The wheel is the map; the
text lives in a calm row below it. Nothing is crowded because the two jobs are
in two places.

**The centre is a plain white disc that overlaps the ring.** It sits ON the
wheel, not inside a hole in it, which is why it reads as the hub rather than as
a gap.

## Where it applies

Directly to the nine-area problem: **shades of one colour rather than
nine hues.** And the split — wheel above, words below — is a way to keep the
board uncluttered while still labelling everything.

## The hard part

Four quadrants of a donut is not nine capsules. Nine slices of one hue
would be nearly indistinguishable; the value steps have to come from somewhere
else, like grouping the areas into families.
