# tubes-light-inside.png

The approved tube direction, chosen 20 September 2026 out of four comps built
from `neumorphic-glow-home.jpg` and `neumorphic-glow-controls.jpg`.

> ממש אהבתי את 1

## Why this folder exists

Everything else in the taste library is someone else's work, pointed at. This
folder is the opposite: a comp of **our** app that has been approved, so it can
be used as the style reference for everything built afterwards. It is published
with the rest of the repo, which is what lets an image model fetch it.

## What it decides

**Four channels pressed into the surface, and the light lives inside them.** Not
a filled bar, not a coloured capsule: a lamp behind frosted white, brightest at
the surface line and fading into the white beside it.

The three comps of the whole screen generated from it are in
`.impeccable/mocks/screen/glow-p-*.png` — panels, no panels, and tiles.

Related: [[neumorphic-glow-home]], [[neumorphic-glow-controls]].
