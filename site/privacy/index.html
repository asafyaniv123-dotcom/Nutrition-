Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Your journal stays on your phone. Hayomi has no account and no sign-in. What you write, eat, wear and photograph is saved on your device, and we have no copy of it.

Some features need a server to work: understanding a meal from a photo, the weekly review, Beem's replies. When you use one of them, the text or photo it needs is sent to process that request. This page lists every one of those cases, what is sent, and how long anything is kept.

1. What stays on your device

Your evening reflections, journal, meals, workouts, closet, people, plans, photos, settings, and your name if you enter one. It is all stored in the app's storage on your phone. We cannot see it, and if you delete the app, it is gone. The backup in settings creates a file you keep; we never receive it.

2. What leaves your device, and why

When you use a feature that understands text, voice or photos

These features send what you give them to our server, which passes it to an AI provider and returns the answer:

  • the coach in Nutrition and the home command line (what you type or say),
  • food photos and nutrition labels,
  • voice input (the recording, to turn it into text),
  • the planner and the planning box,
  • Beem's replies,
  • the weekly review (a summary of that week's entries, so it can write about your week).

The AI providers are Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Gemini). They process the request to answer it. Our server does not store the content of these requests.

When you search for a food

Your search words, or a barcode you scan, are sent to Open Food Facts, an open food database, either directly or through our server.

When you correct a food entry

If you delete or edit a food the app logged, the correction is sent so we can improve how meals are recognised: the food before and after (name, amount, calories and macros), the message you wrote to the coach about it, if any, when it happened, and the random install identifier described below. Kept for 90 days.

Anonymous usage

To know whether the app is working for people, it sends a small record per day: a random install identifier (not linked to you, your device or anything you write), how many times the app was opened, which areas were opened, whether the day was closed, the app's language and the first day the install was seen. Never anything you wrote, ate, named or photographed. Kept for 120 days.

Optional features

  • Evening reminder (web version): to send it, the server keeps a push address from your browser, the time you chose and your time zone, until you turn the reminder off.
  • Steps from a watch: if you connect it, your daily step counts are kept under a random token: the last 14 days, removed after 120 days of no use.
  • Training together: a shared workout keeps the workout plan and the name each person chooses to show, for one day.

Technical data

Like every server, ours sees the IP address of a request. We use it only to apply daily limits on the features above, in counters that expire after two days. Fonts and a few app files are loaded from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, and a YouTube link you add shows a preview fetched from YouTube. Those services see your IP address under their own policies.

4. What we never do

  • No advertising, and no advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your data for marketing.
  • No account, so there is no profile of you on our side.

5. Your choices

  • Not using a feature from section 2 means nothing is sent for it.
  • Turning off the reminder deletes its push address.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device.
  • Since what reaches us is not linked to you, we usually cannot find "your" records. If you believe we hold something about you, write to us and we will look and delete it.

6. Children

Hayomi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything significant will be shown in the app.
