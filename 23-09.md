## How to use this on the 23rd

**The four questions first** — they change what the rest is. Then תזונה,
because it is the one area he has named as breaking the habit, and everything
else in this file belongs to an app he is still opening.

Two things worth carrying in, both learned the hard way this month:

**A green suite is necessary, not sufficient.** The 309-food suite passed
identically on a fold that buried garlic under 499 rows of fat, because it
tests the winner and cannot see the noise behind it.

**Measure before designing.** Three times this week the thing he asked for
turned out to be mostly built already — the `/estimate` endpoint, the RPE row,
the workout duration. Every time, the real gap was narrower than it looked and
in a different place.
