### 1 · Nutrition — needs a Worker deploy, which is his to run

| | |
|---|---|
| **`/see` must read the product NAME** | HERBALIFE 24 was in large letters on the bag and was never read. `/see` hunts for a barcode or a nutrition table, finds neither, and **throws the identity away**. Everything downstream then argues about a brand that is already gone — including the brand rule the `/match` prompt *already has* |
| **A verification pass** | both of today's failures are implausible on their face — an egg at 605 kcal/100g, a "protein supplement" that is 56% carbohydrate. One short second call — *is this plausible for this food at this portion?* — catches both. The cheapest thinking we can buy |
| **Re-order the trust chain** | today: label > table > estimate. **And the open question is now closed by measurement, not opinion.** `/estimate`
is told never to state a brand's published figures. That rule is exactly what
produced the Herbalife answer: unable to say them, the flow fell back to a table
row naming **a different company**. The restraint did not protect him — it
replaced a good estimate with a confident wrong one.
