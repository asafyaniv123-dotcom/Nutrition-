### 4 · Editing a day after it is written

Measured before writing it down, and the gap is narrower than the ask: **a
finished summary can already be reopened — for exactly one day.**
`renderReflectHome` offers **"פתח שוב"** on a completed day and `rfResume()`
drops back into the cards with every answer intact.

But `rfResume()` opens with `_rfDate=todayStr()`, and the day sheet reached
from the Me calendar has **no edit affordance anywhere in it** — the whole of
`renderSummaryOverlay` and `summaryBodyHTML` was read, and there is not a
button, a tap target or a link.

So **yesterday cannot be corrected, and a question can never be added to a day
already summarised** — and a mistake in an evening reflection is usually
noticed the next morning, which is precisely when it stops being editable.

- `rfResume(dateStr)` — every other piece already takes a date; only the entry
  point assumes today.
- A way in **from the day sheet**, because that is where a past day is read.
- The bank, for a past day — the half he named, and the half that is not just
  plumbing. See the fork in §5.

Two things to get right. **The day ends at 04:00**, and editing a past day is
exactly where a raw clock files an answer under the wrong date. And **editing
rewrites history**: the daily summary feeds the week and month cards, the
streak and `trainedOn`, so it should edit an ANSWER rather than re-run the
evening — a reopened day must not be able to come out emptier than it went in.

### 5 · The journal's voice

**It reads like an inbox because it is set in the app's UI font.**
`.jrnl-text{font-family:inherit}` resolves to `Assistant` — the same face as
every button and tab — so a page for private thoughts wears the typography of a
toolbar. It is also one `textarea` on a plain card with a search box under it:
nothing in the layout says *page*.

- **The serif costs nothing.** `--serif` already exists and is used in eleven
  places, from faces that are system or already loaded. Setting the journal in
  it, with a page's proportions, works offline and changes the feeling most.
- **A choice of hand**, remembered per person. Checked rather than assumed:
  **Google Fonts has `Playpen Sans Hebrew`**, built out of handwriting
  research — and the app already loads one webfont exactly this way, so the
  handwritten option is a `<link>`, not a project.
- **His own handwriting, split in two.** A real font from a photographed glyph
  sheet is image processing plus font generation — **a project; cost it before
  promising it.** A photographed PAGE is cheap, more personal than a font
  because it is the actual hand rather than an average of it, and reuses the
  photo pipeline and the closet's tested flood fill.

Two constraints: `sw.js` caches nothing, so a Google-hosted face is lost
offline and must fall back to the serif; and a Hebrew handwriting font does
nothing for the other ten languages, so the setting has to degrade per script.
